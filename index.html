How It Works

Three simple steps to save a life
1

Find an animal that needs help.

Spot a dog or cat on the street needing urgent treatment, temporary shelter, or food. Keep a safe distance and snap a quick photo.

2

Report its location and condition.

Submit a fast 30-second report with the neighborhood spot and urgency tag so nearby rescuers receive immediate notifications.

3

Connect with someone who can help.

Community members, drivers, and local foster volunteers step in directly through one-click contact details to resolve the rescue.
